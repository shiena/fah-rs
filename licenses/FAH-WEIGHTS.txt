on them.

  train methods/algorithms/neural networks/etc. for commercial, pornographic, military, surveillance, or defamatory use
  of any kind." MANO meshes and fits supervise the pose models, the contact heads and the mesh fitters.

  camera-motion head): the same prohibition.
- Training data licensed for non-commercial or research use only: the HOT3D hand annotations (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), DexYCB
  (CC BY-NC 4.0), InterHand2.6M (CC BY-NC 4.0), HOI4D (CC BY-NC 4.0), COCO-WholeBody, EPIC-KITCHENS-100 and VISOR (CC
  BY-NC 4.0), FreiHAND and HanCo, HO-3D, the CMU Panoptic hand labels, EgoDex (CC BY-NC-ND), MOW with HACO's contact
  labels (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- WiLoR (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0): its outputs are distillation targets of the pose models.
- The camera-motion head contains torchvision's pretrained RAFT-small weights, trained on FlyingChairs and FlyingThings3D
  (research use only).

The FAH Weights do not contain or redistribute any of these datasets or the MANO model. MANO is downloaded by the user
under its own licence.
