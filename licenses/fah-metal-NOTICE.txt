This product ports the inference pipeline of FAH (Fast and Accurate Hand estimation,
https://github.com/noahcao/FAH), Copyright 2026 the FAH authors, licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. The detector decoding, crops, network inputs, output decoding, verification, tracking and
MANO per-part Procrustes in Sources/FAHMetal follow FAH's fah/estimator.py, fah/detector.py, fah/coreml.py,
fah/video.py, fah/mano_procrustes.py and bench/methods/fah.py; tools/export.py follows FAH's
scripts/build_coreml.py.

This product contains no FAH model weights and no MANO data. The files tools/export.py writes are derived
from them and stay under their own terms: FAH's WEIGHTS_LICENSE (research and non-commercial use, with the
SAM and DINOv3 pass-through terms) and the MANO licence (https://mano.is.tue.mpg.de/license.html).

Sources/FAHMetal/Shaders/pose.metal adapts code from MLX (https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx): the erf
approximation, the structure of the steel attention and thin tensor-op GEMM kernels. MLX is licensed as
